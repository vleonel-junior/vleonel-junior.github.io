neuro-symbolic learning (Logic Tensor Networks);
mathematical foundations of deep learning: probability, stochastic modeling and optimization.
I am interested in understanding models down to their equations and rebuilding them from first principles.

\section{Research Projects}
\entry{Logic Tensor Networks: presentation and case study}{2026}
      {\href{https://github.com/vleonel-junior/logic-tensor-networks}{github.com/vleonel-junior/logic-tensor-networks}}{}
\details{
  \item Presentation of the neuro-symbolic LTN framework: grounding of logical symbols as tensors, differentiable connectives and quantifiers, knowledge-base satisfaction as a training objective.
  \item Case study with LTNtorch: semi-supervised MNIST digit recognition learned only from the sum of two digits, compared with a purely supervised baseline.
}

\entry{Building a Large Language Model from Scratch: reading notes}{2026}
      {Bilingual (EN/FR) chapter-by-chapter notes on S.~Raschka's book, \href{https://vleonel-junior.github.io/blog/dossier/build-llm-from-scratch/}{published online}}{}
\details{
  \item From-scratch PyTorch implementations of tokenization, byte pair encoding, data sampling, embeddings, self-attention, causal and multi-head attention.
  \item Complementary derivations: softmax Jacobian, the $1/\sqrt{d_k}$ scaling factor, equivalence of causal masking strategies, variance under weight initialization.
}

\entry{Earlier projects}{2025}{Mathematical programming and quantitative finance}{}
\details{
  \item \href{https://github.com/vleonel-junior/Gomory}{Gomory cutting-plane method}: pure Python solver for integer linear programs with exact fractional arithmetic.
  \item \href{https://github.com/vleonel-junior/Black-sholes}{Black--Scholes}: analytical solution through the heat equation, pricing formula and Delta of a European call.
